SECTION 5 · PROPOSED INFORMATION SYSTEMS

Information Systems

Seven connected systems that replace separate applications and manual records.

Core

Hospital Information System

Connects all major departments and centralizes patient and operational information.

  • Centralized database
  • Departmental integration
  • Information sharing

Resolved: One centralized patient record across every department

Clinical

Electronic Medical Records

Stores medical histories, diagnoses, prescriptions and treatments in one digital record.

  • Digital patient records
  • Medical history
  • Prescriptions & treatment records

Resolved: Complete, up-to-date digital patient records

Financial

Billing & Financial Management

Automatically records charges, payments and insurance claims from all services.

  • Automated billing
  • Payment tracking
  • Insurance claim processing

Resolved: Accurate bills and 94% first-pass claim approval

Supply

Inventory Management

Monitors medicines and supplies in real time with expiration and reorder controls.

  • Stock monitoring
  • Expiration tracking
  • Reorder alerts

Resolved: Optimal stock levels with zero shortages or expiry

Diagnostic

Laboratory Information System

Records lab requests and results, then sends completed reports to authorized staff.

  • Laboratory requests
  • Test results
  • Digital reporting

Resolved: Instant lab results delivered straight to the EMR

Operations

Queue Management

Monitors queues, organizes appointments and displays service status to patients.

  • Appointment scheduling
  • Queue numbers
  • Waiting-time monitoring

Resolved: Average patient wait cut to 12 minutes

Management

Management Information System

Generates reports and dashboards for revenue, admissions, beds and department activity.

  • Management dashboards
  • Financial reports
  • Operational analytics

Resolved: Real-time dashboards for faster management decisions

Recommendation

Invest in integration

The proposal recommends one integrated HIS over separate department applications — creating a more connected, efficient, accurate and reliable organization.
